# BIZCASH-30

## AI-Powered Cash Flow Forecasting & Business Finance Analytics

BIZCASH-30 is a business finance analytics system that helps businesses understand their cash flow, analyze financial data, forecast future cash positions, and identify financial situations that may require attention.

## Problem Statement

Small businesses manage financial information such as sales, invoices, bills, expenses, and bank transactions. When this information is difficult to analyze together, businesses may struggle to understand their current cash position and identify possible future cash shortages.

## Proposed Solution

BIZCASH-30 brings business financial data together and analyzes it using data analytics and forecasting techniques.

The system aims to:

- Analyze income and expenses
- Track sales, invoices, bills, and bank transactions
- Analyze historical cash-flow patterns
- Forecast future cash flow
- Identify possible cash shortages
- Generate useful financial insights
- Highlight priority financial actions

## Key Features

- Cash Flow Analysis
- Financial Data Analytics
- Cash Flow Forecasting
