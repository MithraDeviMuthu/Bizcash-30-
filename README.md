# BIZCASH-30

## AI-Powered Cash Flow Forecasting & Business Finance Analytics

BIZCASH-30 is a business finance analytics solution designed to help businesses understand their cash flow, analyze financial transactions, forecast future cash positions, and identify financial areas that require attention.

## Problem Statement

Small businesses manage financial information such as sales, invoices, bills, expenses, and bank transactions.

When these records are difficult to analyze together, businesses may face challenges in:

- Understanding their current cash position
- Tracking income and expenses
- Identifying upcoming cash shortages
- Monitoring pending invoices and bills
- Understanding financial trends
- Making timely financial decisions

## Proposed Solution

BIZCASH-30 brings business financial data together and uses data analytics and forecasting techniques to provide meaningful financial insights.

The system aims to:

- Analyze business income and expenses
- Track sales, invoices, bills, and bank transactions
- Analyze historical cash-flow patterns
- Forecast future cash flow
- Identify possible cash shortages
- Generate financial insights
- Highlight priority financial actions

## Key Features

### 1. Cash Flow Analysis
Analyze cash inflows and outflows to understand the business's financial position.

### 2. Financial Analytics
Analyze sales, invoices, expenses, bills, and bank transactions to identify important financial patterns.

### 3. Cash Flow Forecasting
Use historical financial data to estimate future cash-flow trends.

### 4. Cash Shortage Identification
Identify periods where the projected cash balance may become low.

### 5. Priority Actions
Highlight important financial items that may require attention, such as pending invoices, upcoming bills, and potential cash shortages.

### 6. Financial Dashboard
Present financial information through charts, summaries, and key indicators.
